Oriental is a free, open-source project management tool built for teams in resource-constrained environments. No internet? No problem. Works completely offline and syncs when you're back online.

Get Started Free View on GitHub

Built for Real-World Constraints

Designed from the ground up for teams with limited internet, older devices, and zero budget.

Full Offline Support

Work without internet. All data stored locally in IndexedDB. Automatically syncs when you reconnect. Never lose your work.

Zero Cost Forever

Runs on Firebase's free Spark plan. No credit card needed. 50,000 reads/day is more than enough for most teams. No hidden fees.

Lightweight & Fast

Under 100KB total JavaScript. No heavy frameworks. Works on 2G/3G networks. Loads instantly on any device, even older phones.

Kanban Board

Visual task management with drag-and-drop. Six task states: Planned, Started, Stuck, Review, Completed, Archived. Collapsible columns.

Role-Based Access

Admin, Manager, Team Lead, and Member roles. Each sees exactly what they need. Admins get an overview dashboard with drag-and-drop widgets.

Reports & Analytics

Cumulative flow diagrams, team velocity, task distribution, and time tracking heatmaps. All rendered with lightweight Canvas API - no heavy chart libraries.

Offline-First Architecture

Designed for resilience. Your data lives locally first, then syncs to the cloud when possible.
